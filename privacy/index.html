Privacy

Last changed 18 November 2026. This covers the Bredbord website and the Bredbord Android app.

Without an account

You can use Bredbord without signing in. Your circuits are then kept only on your device (in the browser, or in the app), and nothing about you is sent to us. The site has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

With an account

You sign in with Google. When you do, Bredbord keeps:

  • your email address and Google account ID, which Google gives us so we know it’s you (Google also passes your name and the address of your Google profile photo; they’re kept with your sign-in, but Bredbord doesn’t show or use them);
  • the name you pick, and your picture, which is one of the parts drawings (not a photo of you);
  • your circuits: their titles, descriptions, tags, the circuit itself, and a small picture of it;
  • which circuits you liked, and any reports you make.

We don’t get your Google password, and we don’t read your contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Who can see what

  • Only me: a private circuit is seen only by you.
  • Anyone with the link: anyone you give its link to can open it.
  • Public: anyone can find it in Community, with your name on it.

Your name and picture are public. Your email address is never shown to anyone. The person who runs Bredbord can see reports and can hide shared circuits that break the rules.

Where it’s kept

Accounts and circuits are kept with Supabase, which runs the database and sign-in for Bredbord. The website is served by Vercel. Sign-in goes through Google. We don’t sell or share your information with anyone else.

Deleting your account

In the app or on the website, open your account and choose Delete account. That deletes your account, your name and picture, every circuit you kept (private ones too), their pictures, your likes and your reports, straight away. Remixes other people made of your circuits stay, as theirs, without a link back to yours. Circuits kept only on your device stay there until you delete them.

If you can’t sign in any more, contact us and we’ll delete it for you.
